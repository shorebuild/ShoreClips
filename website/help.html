Help with ShoreClips

What do I download?

The preview is currently a source ZIP, not a production installer. A finished installer will be published only after a successful build and a basic recording test.

Why does macOS ask for permissions?

Screen capture requires macOS Screen Recording permission. If you enable microphone audio, grant microphone permission too. You may need to quit and reopen ShoreClips after changing privacy permissions.

Why isn't F7 saving a clip?

Choose a screen or window, start the replay buffer, and wait for a few seconds of footage. Use the configured global shortcut while the buffer is running. Check that another app isn't claiming that shortcut.

Where are my clips stored?

By default, ShoreClips saves recordings to the ShoreClips folder inside your user Videos folder. Use “Open clips folder” from the app.

Does this capture system/game audio?

The current preview does not provide system/game audio capture on macOS Monterey. Optional microphone capture is separate and requires permission.

Still stuck?

Keep the app version, macOS version, and exact error text when reporting a problem. Do not share private clips or account credentials.
