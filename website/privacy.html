PRIVACY

Your clips stay yours.

ShoreClips is a local-first preview application. This statement describes the current prototype, not future cloud features.

Local recordings

Clips are stored in the ShoreClips folder under your user Videos directory by default. A clip is uploaded to the configured ShoreClips sharing service only when you click Share. Shared clips are publicly viewable by anyone who has the unique URL. Do not share sensitive or private recordings.

Screen and microphone permissions

Screen recording and optional microphone capture use operating-system permissions. Capture starts when a source is available and automatic buffer startup is enabled, or when you start the replay buffer manually.

Future services

Login, cloud sharing, and analytics are not implemented in this preview. If these features are added, this policy must be updated before launch.

Contact

For support, use the repository at github.com/shorebuild/ShoreClips.
